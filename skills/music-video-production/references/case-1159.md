# Case: "11:59" — test clip (2026-09-28) and full MV (2026-09-29)

The first use of this pipeline on a new song. Everything in `examples/1159/` comes from it.

**Song**: original lyrics written for the user (a CUHK student's midnight deadline), generated in Suno
(`v6-mini`, free plan, female lead), 1:54, Opus-in-m4a with embedded timed lyric lines. 130.09 BPM,
four-on-the-floor, drum-less pre-choruses. Clip: first chorus, 29.3 → 45.8 s (16.5 s; the last word is held).

**Concept**: a student's last minute before a deadline, drawn as technical figures, in the P(doom) style
system (ink/bone/one orange, Archivo/Plex Mono/Cormorant, grain). The spark = the upload/progress head.

| Plate | Window | Idea | Key hit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clock` | 28.69–32.84 | split-flap clock; lyric labels its digit groups like dimensions; "running out of time" on a burning time-remaining bar | drop at 30.53: 23:58 → 23:59 slams full-frame on "-nine" |
| `stack` | 32.84–36.99 | bone-paper textbook figure of a call stack; a frame pushed per kick (`coffee()`, `proof_by_induction(n+1)`…) with deadpan values; words stamped into frames | `pop()` refused on "unwind" → `RecursionError: maximum caffeine depth exceeded¹` |
| `submit` | 36.99–40.22 | generic hairline upload portal; words are file chunks on a ruler racing an orange deadline pole | head touches the pole exactly on "line": `SUBMITTED 23:59:59.97 · 30 ms to spare` |
| `bigo` | 40.22–47.14 | graph paper; O(n²) built glyph by glyph from the lyric, the spark climbs one point per word | whip up on "panic", snap down on the 43.45 downbeat onto a flat O(1) line: "fine¹ … ¹ amortized" |

**Process and timings**: setup + analysis ~20 min (including the bugs below); treatment approved by the
user; four authors in parallel, 15–19 min each, 44–70 tool calls, 3–4 look-and-fix rounds each; lead review
sent back two small fixes; cut sheet; final render 4 min 54 s. About an hour end to end.

**Bugs found on the way (now fixed in the scripts)**:
1. Kicks "missing": `SR // 1000 = 44` samples used as 1 ms → 70 ms drift at 30 s.
2. Bar phase half a bar off: the snare band hit every beat (kick click); fixed with drum re-entry votes.
3. The bar was first placed on the pickup "Eleven" instead of the drop "-nine": drops land on downbeats.
4. Suno's line times put chorus 2 ~3 s early: its windows are now only a hint checked against the vocal.
5. No Google Chrome on the machine: `CHROME_PATH` for Chromium.

**Reproducibility check**: a fresh `setup_project.sh` + `song_analysis.py` + the example files rendered the
same frames as the original run (SSIM 0.995, 1.000, 1.000, 0.997 on the four plates' key stills).

**Open for a revision 2**: a moment of its own for "stack"; the speed of `submit`'s tracking camera; the
diagonal "rain" of grid marks during `bigo`'s whip; possibly one 3D plate (a stack tower to orbit).

**Process**: full-song re-analysis (2 fixes to the line-timing rules, now in `song_analysis.py`);
treatment approved with "about half 3D"; real terrain/roads downloaded and the route redrawn by the user
(`references/geo.md`); 12 authoring agents over ~3.5 h, at most 4 at once; 7 review round-trips.

**Review notes that became rules** (see `full-song.md`, `review.md`):
- `night`'s log jokes passed the lens in half a beat → a pinned, readable log panel.
- `canteen`'s last word "dream" had 5 frames before the cut → cut half a beat later.
- `clock2`'s post `flash` greyed the whole ink frame → an exposure kick on the flaps and a rim light instead.
- `bus` v2: a covered walkway stood over the lyric's road → drop small footprints on that stretch only.
- The outro was built before `bigo2` existed and guessed the hand-off → pass exact screen positions across cuts.
- A brief sent to the wrong agent ("ignore this") made it stop; the real brief had to be resent.
- The first full render timed out at boot (120 s) → the boot timeout is now 10 min.

**Render**: 1080p60, adaptive motion blur, 40 min on an M4; master 842 MB, share copy 270 MB;
audio mean −16.3 dB, peak −0.6 dB.
