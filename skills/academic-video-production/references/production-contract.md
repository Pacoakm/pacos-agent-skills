# Production contract

Keeps the notes, the plan, the scenes, the captions, the Palmier timeline and the export in
agreement.

## Project layout

Default root: `~/academic-videos/<COURSE>/<nn>-<slug>/`, unless Paco names another place.

```text
~/academic-videos/<COURSE>/<nn>-<slug>/
├── notes/                   # the source lecture files (PDF / PPTX), or notes/SOURCE.md
│                            # holding their absolute paths if they stay where they are
├── notes-map.md             # Gate 1: concepts with slide numbers, notation, verbatim
│                            # definitions/theorems, what the slides skip, suspected errors
├── brief.md                 # per knowledge point: picture, prerequisites, wrong model, aha,
│                            # central animation, examples, formal statement, justifications,
│                            # tags, opening, limits, colour assignments, fact check
├── video-plan.json          # THE timing authority
├── storyboard/
│   ├── manifest.json
│   ├── frames/              # one per shot — Manim stills from the real scenes,
│   │                        # never hand-authored SVG mockups
│   └── sheets/              # 4K review sheets (SVG document furniture only)
├── src/
│   ├── script.py            # the scenes; imports the theme via theme_boot
│   └── captions.py          # generated by build_captions.py — do not hand-edit, not rendered
├── audio/
│   └── voice/               # ONLY if a voice was asked for: edge-tts files, one per shot
│                            # or knowledge point
└── out/
    ├── concat-draft.txt
    ├── draft-picture.mp4    # 480p15 concatenation, before the subtitle mux
    ├── draft.mp4            # the same picture + SOFT subtitle track — what Paco
    │                        # watches at the Gate 3 approval stop
    ├── subtitles.srt        # bilingual sidecar, written by build_captions.py
    ├── subtitles-zh.srt     # split for Palmier's 中文 caption track (Gate 4)
    ├── subtitles-en.srt     # split for Palmier's English caption track (Gate 4)
    └── final.mp4            # THE MASTER — exported from Palmier Pro, never built here
```

The Palmier project (`.palmier`) lives wherever Palmier saves it; its path is recorded in
`assembly.project`. There is no `picture.mp4`, no caption overlay render and no ffmpeg master:
Gate 4 is assembled in Palmier and `out/final.mp4` is its export
(`references/palmier-assembly.md`).

## Invariants

Check these; do not assume them.

1. Shots sorted by `start`; first `start` is `0`; last `end` equals `durationSeconds`.
   `durationSeconds` is whatever the shots add up to — an output, never a target. There is no
   minimum and no maximum (rule 39).
2. No gaps, no overlaps; `end > start` for every shot.
3. Every shot duration is a whole number of frames at `fps`. At 60 fps any multiple of 0.05 s works.
4. Subtitle cues lie inside their shot, do not overlap, and each lasts ≥ 2.0 s.
5. For each shot: `字數 ≤ (end − start) × 4.0` on the **中文** line, counting a Latin word as 2 字.
   The English line is length-limited, not rate-limited — see `narration-and-subtitles.md`.
5b. **Every cue has both languages.** `text` is 繁體中文書面語 and carries no English word except
   an ALL-CAPS abbreviation (`DFS`), a single letter (`A`, `x`) or a listed notation (`pH`,
   `O(n)`); counts and measurements in it are Arabic numerals (`2 個`, not `兩個`). `en` is the
   same sentence in the lecture's English, with the course's terms verbatim, **on one line**.
   Limits in 16:9: 中文 24 全形字, two lines maximum; English 90 characters on one line.
   `build_captions.py` checks all of it, and additionally lays every cue out to count its real
   lines and measure it against the reserved caption band.
6. For each shot: `stillSeconds ≥ (end − start) × 0.25`.
7. Shot IDs are stable after storyboard approval.
7b. `theme` is `"dark"` or `"light"` and is the answer Paco gave at Gate 1. A light-theme video
   calls `sq.use_light()` before building anything; see `references/brand-theme.md`, "The light
   theme". Discovering the wrong one at Gate 4 costs the whole picture master.
7c. `format` is always `"long"`, `aspect` `"16:9"`, `width` × `height` 1920 × 1080, `fps` 60.
   There are no shorts in this skill.
8. `register` is `"math"` or `"verbal"` — the shot speaks one of them, never both. A figure is
   welcome in either.
   - `"math"` — equations, derivations, bounds, pseudo-code. `onScreenText`'s
     **non-mathematical** entries (labels, term cards) are kept as short as the idea allows — no
     fixed word cap — and none is a sentence. Mathematics, pseudo-code and justifications are
     exempt and uncounted.
   - `"verbal"` — one enumerated list of steps, conditions or cases: ≤ 5 items, each ≤ 8 words
     and one line, and **no displayed equation** anywhere in the shot. Inline symbols inside an
     item (`⊥`, `∈`, `A_{ij}`) do not count and do not make it a formula.

   Loose explanatory sentences — anything that is not an item of an enumerated list — are banned
   in both. Every sentence that is *not* on the frame has an entry in `movedToNarration` saying
   where it went (with no voice, that is the caption track). See `references/on-screen-language.md`.
9. `givens` lists what is established when the shot opens — what the notes or the stated problem
   give, or what an earlier shot built; those are the only marks on the figure at its first
   frame. Every **derived** quantity has a `beats` entry, and each beat names all three strands
   that land together in one `play()`: the derivation `line`, the `figure` event it produces, and
   the subtitle `cue` index that explains it. A beat's `at` lies inside its shot and inside its
   cue's span. No derived quantity appears on the figure before its beat.
   See `references/on-screen-language.md`.
10. A scene's end state equals the next scene's start state — figure **and** on-screen text.
    Build both from a shared helper so they cannot drift.
11. `narration.source` is `"none"` unless Paco asked for a voice on this video. If it is
    `"edge-tts"`, `narration.media` holds the files, and `narration.status` is
    `"audio-received"` only once every file exists and is placed on Palmier's `Voice` track.
    Never describe a video as narrated while `source` is `"none"` (rule 2).
11b. `assembly.route` is always `"palmier"`. `assembly.project` holds the `.palmier` path once
    Gate 4 has created it. `captions.burnedIn` is `false` and `captions.track` is `null` — no
    `captions.py` is rendered; the captions are live caption clips, burned only by the Palmier
    export.
11c. **One chapter per knowledge point.** `assembly.chapters` has exactly one entry per entry of
    `knowledgePoints`, in order; its `name` is that knowledge point's `sectionTag`, verbatim; its
    `firstShot` is the knowledge point's first shot (its opening); its `startFrame` is that
    shot's clip start frame on the Palmier timeline. The markers placed with `manage_markers`
    match this list one-for-one.
12. **A worked problem carries its statement.** If the video works one — from the notes, a
    tutorial sheet or a homework — `question.stem` holds it verbatim in the source's English as
    LaTeX, and every shot of that example names a `questionPart` that exists in
    `question.parts`. A shot with a `questionPart` renders the stem **and** that part; no shot
    renders a part without the stem, and no two consecutive shots of one example disagree about
    the stem. The stem is exempt from invariant 8's budget and from the one-register rule;
    nothing else on those shots is. See `references/on-screen-language.md`, "The question band".
13. `out/draft.mp4` has a subtitle stream. It is built by muxing `out/subtitles.srt` into
    `out/draft-picture.mp4` as **soft** `mov_text` — never burned in — and `ffprobe
    -select_streams s` confirms it before the draft is shown at the Gate 3 stop.
14. **Every example has a role, and each knowledge point's first example is concrete.** Each
    entry in `examples` names its `knowledgePoint`, a `role` of `"concrete"`, `"varied"`,
    `"broken"` or `"connected"`, and `shows` — one sentence naming what it makes visible. The
    first example of every knowledge point is `"concrete"` and comes before the general
    statement. There is no exam ladder, no rung, no past-paper framing (rule 28).
    See `references/lesson-patterns.md`.
15. **A computation's first line is general.** Where an example works a computation, the first
    entry of its `lines`, and the first line the animated solve writes, is the formula in
    symbols with none of the example's numbers in it. The substitution is the second line.
    Built with `derivation()`, whose first argument is the general form. See rule 27.
16. **A recap page is optional.** An example may end on a still page (`"solutionPage": true` on
    its last shot) when the computation is long enough that Paco would want to pause on it. If it
    does, that shot has no `beats`, its `stillSeconds` covers the whole shot, it is at least
    `REST_RECAP` (4.0 s), and it renders the example's `lines` with `solution_page()`. No
    example is required to have one.
17. **Every string on the picture is English.** Every entry of every shot's `onScreenText`, every
    `sectionTitle`, every `sectionTag`, the `question` block, and each example's `lines` carry no
    CJK character. The **only** fields in the plan that may hold 中文 are `subtitles[].text`,
    `openings[].plainWord`, and the notes in `movedToNarration`. Grep the plan for CJK outside
    those before the Gate 2 render — the theme raises at build time, but catching it in the plan
    is a minute instead of a render. See rule 29.
18. **Shot 1 is the title card.** `shots[0]` has `"titleCard": true`, is 3.0–4.0 s long, has no
    `beats`, no `questionPart` and no `ponder`, and its `stillSeconds` covers the whole shot. It
    is built with `title_card()` from `titleCard.topic` and `titleCard.subtitle` — the subtitle
    being exactly `[course, "Lecture N"]` — and its `onScreenText` is exactly those two lines,
    the subtitle joined with ` · `. It is the one shot exempt from invariant 8's register rule:
    a title card speaks neither register. No other shot sets `titleCard`. The opening is
    `shots[1]`. See rule 30.
19. **Every knowledge point opens on something Paco can see before it is named.** Each entry of
    `openings` names the `shot` that shows the concrete case, question or puzzle, what it
    `varies` to make the behaviour visible, the `plainWord` (中文, caption only) and the `term` it
    bridges to, plus the `termFirstShot` where that term first appears. `termFirstShot` comes
    **after** `shot`, and the term appears nowhere earlier — not in an `onScreenText`, not in a
    `subtitles[].en`, not in a section tag. One entry per knowledge point. See rule 31.
20. **Every shot names its knowledge point.** Every shot that is not the title card and not an
    end card carries `sectionTitle` — its knowledge point's `sectionTag`, in the lecture's
    English, four words and 32 characters at most, rendered top-left by `Stage.section_tag()`.
    Every shot of one knowledge point carries the **identical** string, which makes it part of
    invariant 10's end state; it changes only where the knowledge point changes, and it appears
    no earlier than that knowledge point's `termFirstShot`, because a tag *is* a section title
    and invariant 19 bans the term from one. A shot that deliberately carries no tag — the
    opening shots before the bridge — says `"sectionTitle": null`, so a bare frame is visibly a
    decision rather than an omission. See rule 34.
21. **Every knowledge point traces to the notes.** Each entry of `knowledgePoints` has a
    `slides` range inside a `sourceNotes` entry, and `notes-map.md` lists it. Every entry of
    `justifications` is a definition, theorem or lemma named the way the notes name it, with
    where it is. A statement that is not in the notes is flagged in `brief.md`, never silently
    added (rule 3).

Invariant 10 is the one that bites. Scenes render independently, so a mismatch produces a jump
cut that is invisible at draft resolution and obvious in the master.

## Render commands

```bash
export PATH="$HOME/Library/TinyTeX/bin/universal-darwin:$PATH"   # if MathTex is used
rm -rf media/Tex media/texts    # after ANY font or TeX-template change — the Tex
                                # SVG cache is keyed by the expression, not the preamble

# Gate 3 — draft; review pacing here, not resolution
python3 tools/render.py draft          # renders what is stale, stitches, muxes the soft SRT
# which is, by hand:
manim -ql src/script.py S01Title S02Opening ...
ffmpeg -y -f concat -safe 0 -i out/concat-draft.txt -c copy out/draft-picture.mp4
python3 scripts/build_captions.py --plan video-plan.json --out-dir src
ffmpeg -y -i out/draft-picture.mp4 -i out/subtitles.srt \
  -c:v copy -c:s mov_text -metadata:s:s:0 language=zho -disposition:s:0 default \
  out/draft.mp4
ffprobe -v error -select_streams s -show_entries stream=index,codec_name -of csv=p=0 out/draft.mp4
                                                                           # then SHOW Paco

# Gate 4 — the scenes at master quality, for Palmier. No concat, no caption render.
manim -r 1920,1080 --fps 60 src/script.py S01Title S02Opening ...
# → assemble in Palmier Pro: references/palmier-assembly.md

# Gate 5 — after Palmier has exported out/final.mp4, on Paco's word
python3 scripts/verify_master.py --plan video-plan.json --master out/final.mp4 \
  --scene-dir media/videos/script/1080p60        # add --require-audio only with a voice
```

Measured throughput, M-series Mac, 1080p60: about **39 frames/second**, so ≈8 min of rendering
for a 5-minute video and ≈30 min for a 20-minute one. 2560×1440 measured 21.8 fps if a 2K master
is ever wanted.

## Status vocabulary

Use these exact strings in `video-plan.json` so a resumed session knows where it is:

`planning` → **`plan-awaiting-approval`** → `plan-approved` →
**`storyboard-awaiting-approval`** → `storyboard-approved` →
**`draft-awaiting-approval`** → `draft-approved` →
`awaiting-export` → `delivered`

The three bold states are the mandatory approval stops. Each means: the artifact has been shown
to Paco and the session is stopped. Only his explicit approval moves it to the matching
`*-approved` state — never your own review, never silence, never "it looks right".

`awaiting-export` is set when the Palmier timeline is assembled, captioned, chaptered and
inspected. It is not an approval stop but it is a stop: it waits for Paco to say export, or to
export himself. `delivered` is set only when `out/final.mp4` exists, came out of Palmier, and has
passed `verify_master.py`. A finished timeline is never `delivered`, and any timeline change
after an export sends the status back to `awaiting-export`.

Never advance the status past what actually exists on disk, and never past what Paco has
actually approved.

## Approval-stop artifacts

Each stop has one artifact that must reach Paco's eyes, not a prose summary of it.

| Status | Artifact shown | How |
|---|---|---|
| `plan-awaiting-approval` | The concept map, the full per-shot caption script with 字數 and pacing verdicts, the notation and flags from `notes-map.md` | In the reply text and the dashboard; `notes-map.md`, `brief.md`, `captions.md` and `video-plan.json` on disk for detail |
| `storyboard-awaiting-approval` | `storyboard/frames/*.png` in the dashboard, `storyboard/sheets/*.png` | Dashboard; `SendUserFile` or exact paths for the sheets |
| `draft-awaiting-approval` | `out/draft.mp4` — picture **plus its soft subtitle track** | Dashboard and `SendUserFile`, or exact paths if unavailable. Say the track may need turning on, and name `out/subtitles.srt` as the fallback |

A revision at any stop regenerates the artifact and shows it again — a re-rendered draft for a
pacing note, re-rendered stills for a composition note, an updated plan and script for a wording
note. Then stop again.
